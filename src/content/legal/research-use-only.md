# Research Use Only Policy

PepCore Labs sells research compounds exclusively for **laboratory and research purposes**. By purchasing from this site, you confirm and agree to all of the following.

## Who may purchase

- You are **18 years of age or older**.
- You are a **qualified researcher**, laboratory professional, or institution conducting legitimate scientific research, or purchasing on behalf of one.
- You will not use, or allow any other person to use, any product purchased from PepCore Labs for human consumption, medical treatment, veterinary use, cosmetic application, or any household purpose.

## What "Research Use Only" means

- Products are supplied strictly for **in-vitro and laboratory research** — not for diagnostic, therapeutic, or any clinical purpose in humans or animals.
- No content on this site, including product descriptions, testing information, or FAQ answers, constitutes medical advice, a dosing recommendation, or an endorsement of any use outside a laboratory research setting.
- Any resemblance between a product name and a substance studied in human or veterinary medicine does not imply this product is approved, tested, or intended for that use.

## Testing and documentation

Every batch is analyzed by an independent laboratory before release (see [Testing & Analysis](/#testing)). At this time, PepCore Labs provides general purity/testing information rather than a downloadable Certificate of Analysis (COA) per lot; lot-specific documentation is available on request and will be published directly on product pages once that process is in place (see the project roadmap).

## Shipping scope

Products currently ship within the EU27 member states. Shipping to a destination is not, by itself, a determination that a given product is lawful to import, possess, or use there — that determination is the purchaser's responsibility.

## Enforcement

PepCore Labs reserves the right to refuse or cancel any order it reasonably believes is intended for human, veterinary, or household use, or that violates this policy.
