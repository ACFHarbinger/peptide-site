# Shipping & Returns

## Shipping scope

We currently ship to all **EU27 member states**. Shipping to a given country does not, by itself, confirm that a specific product may lawfully be imported, possessed, or used there — that determination is the purchaser's responsibility. See our [Research Use Only Policy](/legal/research-use-only).

## Dispatch times

Orders placed before 3pm CET are typically dispatched the same business day. EU delivery generally takes 2–5 business days depending on destination.

## Packaging

All orders ship in discreet, temperature-stable packaging with no external branding indicating contents. Lyophilized vials are shipped ambient unless a product page states otherwise.

## Free shipping

Orders meeting the free-shipping threshold displayed in your cart qualify for free same-day dispatch shipping automatically — no code needed.

## Returns

Because products are supplied for laboratory research use and are shipped in tamper-evident packaging, we can only accept returns for:

- Items damaged in transit (contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage), or
- Items shipped in error on our part.

Contact [support@pepcorelabs.io](mailto:support@pepcorelabs.io) with your order number to start a return or report an issue. Refunds, once approved, are issued to the original payment method via our checkout provider.

## Lost or delayed shipments

If a shipment appears lost or significantly delayed, contact us and we will investigate with the carrier and, where appropriate, arrange a reshipment or refund.
